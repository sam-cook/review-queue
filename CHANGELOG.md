## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added

- Menu bar app that lists the pull requests needing your attention: review requests,
  re-reviews, new commits or replies after your review, teammates' unreviewed PRs, and
  unaddressed feedback on your own PRs.
- Waiting and Dismissed sections; dismiss until something changes, or hide for good.
- Count badge that turns red for new items, and macOS notifications.
- Editable team list, checked against GitHub.
- github.com by default; GitHub Enterprise Server through the `host` setting.
- `--dump`, `--demo` and `--snapshot` command-line modes.
